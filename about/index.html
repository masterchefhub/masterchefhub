Our story

Why MasterChefHub exists.

MasterChefHub started in a home kitchen with a simple idea: everyday food, cooked fresh, without the fuss. No fancy restaurant menu, just the meals people actually want, like a hot sandwich, a bowl of pasta or a slice of cake.

You choose your food on the site, enter your address, and we bring it to you. You do not pay online. You pay at the door, in cash or with Venmo, when your order arrives.

  • Cooked at home, made to order
  • Delivered to your front door
  • Pay cash or Venmo at the door
